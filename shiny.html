How Gender & Natures Work in the Gen 4 Cute Charm Glitch

1. The Gender Mechanic (Lead vs. Target)
Cute Charm activates on 2/3 (66.67%) of wild encounters and forces the wild Pokémon to be the opposite gender of your lead:
· Male Lead (Recommended): Targets Females and works across ALL mixed-gender species (12.5% F, 25% F, 50% F, 75% F).
· Female Lead: Targets Males, and your TID/SID only works for one specific gender ratio (87.5% M, 75% M, 50% M, or 25% M).
· Does not work on genderless, 100% male/female species, or static Method 1 encounters (except wild Heatran).
2. How Natures Are Grouped
When Cute Charm triggers, the game forces 1 of 25 fixed PIDs (one per nature). Your TID + SID only unlocks one Shiny Group at a time — meaning picking a Nature you want also gives you all the other natures in that same group (up to 8 natures = 21.33% shiny rate).
3. Does Your Lead Pokémon's Nature/PID Matter?
No! Your Cute Charm lead Pokémon can have any PID, Nature, or IVs (even traded) — only its Cute Charm ability and Gender matter.
